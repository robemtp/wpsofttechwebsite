What's Different

What makes ScribeRun different

Not a feature checklist against someone else's product — the things we deliberately built differently, and why.

01

🧠 The AI reads your protocols

Most documentation AI writes generic prose. ScribeRun is built to take your agency's own protocol set as its reference, so the narrative reflects the standard you actually operate under.

02

✅ Every chart reviewed, not a sample

QA by sampling is a staffing compromise, not a choice. Automated review is designed to run on every chart, which is an architectural decision rather than a headcount one.

03

💵 Billing requirements built into the chart

Documentation gaps that cause denials get flagged while the crew is still in the chart — not weeks later when a biller kicks it back.

04

🚑 Built by someone who ran the calls

Twenty years on an ambulance, ten of them in the oilfield. Every screen was designed by someone who has written a report at 3am after a bad call — not by a product team interviewing medics.
